## Description

Voltline is a native iPhone scooter-driving game built around an electrical and mechanical simulation rather than hidden arcade speed boosts.

Ride the Maxshot-inspired starter scooter through a night district with traffic, AI riders, a tunnel, slalom landmarks, and multiple camera views. Throttle, braking, steering, tire grip, voltage sag, battery state, current limits, motor temperature, controller cutoffs, road load, rider lean, and crashes all feed the same simulation shown on the dashboard.

Open the in-game PhoneOS to view maps and weather, message characters, capture ride photos, track bank deposits, order compatible upgrades, inspect scooter telemetry, and tune a smart FOC controller. Distance driven creates earnings and delivery progress. A persistent objective board rewards checkpoint routes, distance, speed, clean riding, deposits, photography, and hardware upgrades.

Voltline includes touch controls, supported game-controller input, first-run onboarding, 30/60/120 FPS presentation modes, automatic thermal protection, reduced motion, procedural ride audio, haptics, and lifecycle-safe pausing.

Features:

- Deterministic scooter drivetrain and road-load simulation
- Chase, close, and true POV cameras
- Touch and controller riding controls
- Night district, traffic, AI scooter riders, and crash behavior
- Garage, compatible parts, deliveries, and permanent progression
- Draggable PhoneOS with telemetry, Bank, Market, Camera, Photos, and tuning
- Seven physics-backed objectives with real checkpoint navigation
- Player-selectable performance modes and thermal protection

Voltline is a game. Do not copy dangerous riding behavior on real roads.
